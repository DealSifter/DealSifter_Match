# Statewide-first P0 audit

See p0-source-review.json for each state's technology, fields, actual-use review, intended acquisition mode, important gaps and primary source URLs. official-discovery.json is metadata-only discovery, NOT verified authority/coverage; irrelevant search hits are not counted as official sources. statewide-metadata.json records the actual official layer schema. county-inventory.json provides the current Census denominator (3144 county equivalents, 50 states + DC; no territories). runtime-source-validation.json records bounded real queries, including failures. No population/housing coverage percentage was measured.

Arkansas is the activated statewide source: official polygon metadata expressly permits map use; runtime point/APN/lens queries passed in Pulaski/Benton. The separate authority aggregate inventory found APN-bearing positive-area polygon records in every one of the 75 counties. This is registered source/data presence, NOT full parcel coverage: the authority specifically warns that some counties contain partial blocks and CAMA/geometry gaps. Johns Creek is a licensed, verified city subset of Fulton, not a full-county or statewide source. Attribute coverage metrics measure source support, never every property's field completeness.

Florida and Ohio remain runtime-blocked: record queries exceeded the unchanged 7.5-second budget even after one bounded JSON/filter alternative. Longer county-count diagnostics also failed for Ohio. They are not activated merely because terms/schema look good. Texas's official StratMap service schema and state DataHub pathway exist, but intended hosted-use terms were not resolved; no records were mined. PASDA is incomplete and its PIN definition/county-level terms need reconciliation. Los Angeles's current official distribution is bulk-download oriented and incorporates separate EGIS terms; public display approval is not inferred from reachability. Michigan explicitly restricts its statewide layer to internal use. Restricted/paid-only sources are not queried/purchased.

Existing public candidates NC OneMap, Utah LIR, MassGIS and NJOGIS were retained only with reviewed public-use terms, independent source mappings and fresh successful bounded runtime samples. Vermont remains registered ACTIVE for its earlier internal validation but is excluded from GA by INTERNAL_ANALYTICS_ONLY; public-display approval has not been silently upgraded.
