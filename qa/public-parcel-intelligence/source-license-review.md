## Discovery catalog

`phase1b-discovery.json` records public catalog candidates, publisher hints, terms links and schema scores. These are **unverified discovery results**, including unrelated layers returned by broad catalog search. Their review status is UNKNOWN until matched to the manually reviewed table above. They cannot become runtime sources through discovery. Each candidate's item URL is its terms/license review link; no unsupported assertion of official authority is made from a title or score alone.

## Promotion decision

Only the MassGIS, NJOGIS, VCGI, NC OneMap, specific Salt Lake LIR and current FGIO candidates may be considered for promotion after live point, APN and viewport records have been documented. No promotion follows solely from this review. Original Wake County endpoint is not promoted by virtue of an alternative state service working. Tested county coverage is explicit, not inferred national/statewide coverage.
