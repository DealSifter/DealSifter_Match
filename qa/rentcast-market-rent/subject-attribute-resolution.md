# Subject resolution

Use normalized public-authorized address and a fresh reconciliation of current
publisher row, current authorized provider-record cache and retained same-identity
public evidence. Only current publisher decisions become shared overrides. Private
investor scenarios are not subject attributes. KEEP/ACCEPT must match current
evidence hash; REVIEW_LATER cannot clear a material conflict. Original alternatives
remain historical evidence.

Missing optional attributes are omitted with lookupSubjectAttributes=true. A
material unresolved type/beds/baths/living-area disagreement returns the exact review
field and zero provider calls. The existing owner action opens Edit/Page2/field.
No new review drawer/modal. Current inputs are revalidated under lease and after
the provider response; changed inputs cannot consume/store an old matching result.

Returned AVM subject attributes never write the property. Non-echoed property-scope
attributes may enter the existing explicit reconciliation CHECK as estimated
RentCast evidence, not independent verified records. Unit-scope metadata cannot
become building facts. Page2 open/radio changes do not initiate this CHECK.
